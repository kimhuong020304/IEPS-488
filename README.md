This course develops skills in strategic management, innovation, and sustainable leadership. Students will explore organizational design, corporate governance, and financial strategy through case studies and practical exercises. The course enhances critical thinking and problem-solving abilities, with a strong emphasis on ethical decision-making and sustainable
practices. Students will improve communication and teamwork through collaborative projects, preparing to tackle business challenges, drive growth, and contribute to sustainable development in the global business landscape.
At the end of this course, students will be able to:

- Demonstrate their knowledge in strategic management.
- Apply concepts and steps of strategic planning to real life projects from a startup/SME.
- Generate competitive recommendations to enhance value for start-ups/SMEs
